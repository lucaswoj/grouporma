Group ORMA — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-19

Short version

Group ORMA does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All assessment data stays on your device. Deleting the app deletes the data.

What the app stores

The app stores your in-progress and recent assessments (participant count, votes, notes) locally on your device using the standard iOS preferences store. Nothing leaves the device.

Network

The app makes no network requests. There are no analytics, no crash reporters, no third-party SDKs.

Sharing

The app includes a Share button that uses the standard iOS share sheet. Anything shared this way is initiated by you and goes only to the destination you pick (Messages, Mail, AirDrop, etc.).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL.
